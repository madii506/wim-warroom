05 · FAQ

Questions from the bunker.

What is WIM?

A war room for prediction markets. It pulls every live Polymarket market on strikes, ceasefires, invasions and other conflicts onto one screen: a map of the fronts, a live board, charts, order books and the tape.

Does WIM hold my money?

No. WIM has no wallet and no account system. Every bet is placed on Polymarket from your own account, and SOL deposits go through Polymarket's own bridge to your own Polymarket balance.

Where do the odds come from?

Straight from Polymarket's public APIs, in your browser. Prices are what traders are paying right now; the board refreshes every minute and the tape every 20 seconds.

How do I place a bet?

Hit a YES or NO button. It opens that exact market on Polymarket, where you confirm the trade from your own account.

Can I use SOL?

Yes. Paste your Polymarket wallet address in Fund with SOL to get your personal Solana deposit address from Polymarket's bridge. Send at least about $2 worth.

Can everyone bet?

Polymarket decides who can trade, and it restricts some countries. Check its terms where you live. WIM does not help anyone get around them.
